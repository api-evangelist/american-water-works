# American Water Works

> American Water Works Company (NYSE: AWK) is the largest publicly traded U.S. water and
> wastewater utility, serving roughly 14 million people in more than 1,700 communities across
> 14 states through regulated subsidiaries, plus water and wastewater systems on U.S. military
> installations under long-term privatization contracts. American Water publishes NO public
> API, NO developer program, and NO machine-readable contract of any kind. This file is
> generated by API Evangelist from the catalog profile; it is not published by American Water.

## What American Water does NOT publish

- No OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL SDL, AsyncAPI or Postman collection. Probed 2026-09-02:
  `/openapi.json`, `/swagger.json`, `/api-docs` on www.amwater.com all return HTTP 404.
- No developer portal. `developer.amwater.com` and `api.amwater.com` do not resolve.
- No MCP server, no A2A agent card (`/.well-known/agent-card.json` and `/.well-known/agent.json`
  both 404 on www.amwater.com).
- No first-party SDK in any package registry. The GitHub organization
  https://github.com/American-Water exists but has zero public repositories.
- IMPORTANT for readers of this file: the "Developers" pages on amwater.com
  (e.g. /caaw/About-Us/Doing-Business-With-Us/Developers) are for LAND AND PROPERTY
  developers requesting water main extensions for new construction. They are not software
  developer documentation, and they are the most common false positive on this company.

## Agent access posture

- https://www.amwater.com/robots.txt — `User-agent: ClaudeBot` and `User-agent: Claude-Web`
  are both `Disallow: /` (full-site block), while `User-agent: *` is `Allow: /`. American Water
  disallows named AI crawlers specifically while permitting general crawlers.

## Customer surfaces (human, not programmatic)

- [MyWater](https://login.amwater.com/): the customer self-service portal — bill payment,
  account management, usage, start/stop service, alert enrollment. An Angular single-page
  application behind a login; there is no documented API and no public contract behind it.
- Advanced Metering Infrastructure (AMI) collects hourly consumption, but American Water does
  not publish a customer data-download or third-party data-sharing API for it.
